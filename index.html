READY TO EAT APP

Revolutionizing food pre-booking for travelers

Problem

Users struggle to pre-book food while traveling, leading to long wait times and uncertainty about food availability at stations and stops.

Solution

Designed a seamless booking and pre-order experience that allows travelers to order food in advance, track preparation status, and collect meals on arrival.
